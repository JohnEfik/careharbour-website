We are a Canadian healthcare and community services organization committed to delivering person-centred support to individuals and families across different stages of life. Care is more than meeting basic needs. It is about recognizing individual strengths, respecting personal choices, supporting independence, and creating environments where people experience dignity, connection, and belonging.

What We Do

Care and Support That Recognize Every Individual

People have different needs, experiences, and circumstances. Our services are designed around the belief that care should be responsive, respectful, and centred on the individual.

Supporting independence. Encouraging belonging. Recognizing every ability.

Residential & Developmental Care

Individuals with developmental disabilities deserve opportunities to live fulfilling lives in environments that respect their individuality, choices, and abilities. Our services focus on supportive living, personal development, community participation, and individualized assistance.

  • Safe and supportive residential environments
  • Individualized support based on personal needs and preferences
  • Assistance with activities of daily living, where required
  • Development of life skills and independence
  • Social inclusion and meaningful community participation
  • Respect for individual rights, dignity, and personal choices
  • Collaboration with families, caregivers, and relevant professionals

Our goal is to support individuals to experience a sense of home, purpose, independence, and belonging.

Supporting children. Strengthening families. Building positive foundations.

Child & Family Services

Children deserve safe, nurturing, and supportive environments that encourage healthy development and positive relationships. We focus on promoting children’s well-being, supporting families, and recognizing the importance of stable, caring environments.

  • Childcare and early childhood development
  • Safe and supportive care environments
  • Family-centred support
  • Child development and well-being
  • Foster care services, where appropriately licensed and authorized
  • Collaboration with families and community service providers

Supporting children also means supporting the families and communities around them.

Restoring confidence. Supporting recovery. Encouraging independence.

Rehabilitation & Recovery

Recovery is a personal journey. Every individual deserves support that recognizes their circumstances, strengths, and goals. We help individuals regain confidence, develop functional abilities, and improve their quality of life.

  • Rehabilitation and recovery support
  • Assistance with daily living and functional independence
  • Individualized recovery goals
  • Transitional care and supportive environments
  • Collaboration with healthcare professionals and community partners
  • Support for individuals adjusting to changes in health, function, or living circumstances

Meaningful recovery involves more than physical progress. It includes emotional well-being, social connection, and confidence.

Honouring experience. Preserving dignity. Supporting quality of life.

Seniors’ Care

Older adults deserve care that recognizes their life experiences, personal preferences, and changing needs. Our seniors’ care is guided by a commitment to dignity, comfort, safety, and meaningful engagement.

  • Supportive living and residential care
  • Assistance with daily activities, where required
  • Social engagement and meaningful activities
  • Respect for individual preferences and routines
  • Family and caregiver involvement
  • Support for independence, connection, and quality of life

Ageing should be approached with respect, compassion, and recognition of the individual behind every care need.

All services are subject to applicable licensing, regulatory approvals, operational requirements, and service availability.

Who We Are

Redefining What Care Means. Creating Places Where Everyone Belongs.

Across every stage of life, individuals and families deserve care environments where dignity is respected, abilities are recognized, and individual needs are understood. Yet finding the right support can be challenging, particularly for families navigating developmental disabilities, childcare needs, rehabilitation, or support for older adults.

CareVolution exists to help bridge that gap. We bring together compassion, accountability, inclusion, and a commitment to quality, because every individual has a story, every family has unique circumstances, and every person deserves to live with purpose, independence, and a sense of belonging.

Driven by Values. United by Care. Committed to Excellence.

Our Vision

To build inclusive communities where every individual, regardless of age or ability, is empowered to live with safety, purpose, belonging, and meaningful connection in a place they can call home.

We Are Committed

Our Mission

To provide safe, supportive, and person-centred living environments where individuals of all ages and abilities receive compassionate care, experience dignity and respect, and are supported to grow, heal, and live as independently as possible within their communities.

Every individual matters. Every story deserves to be heard. Every person deserves care that recognizes their dignity, respects their choices, and values their potential.
Our ALIVE Values

Our Core Values

The Values That Define Us

Our values shape how we make decisions, how we treat people, how we deliver services, and how we work together. ALIVE is the foundation of our culture and our commitment to care.

  1. A

    Accountability

    We take responsibility for our decisions, actions, and outcomes, committed to ethical conduct, transparency, reliability, and maintaining trust.

  2. L

    Leadership

    Leadership is shown through responsibility, integrity, and initiative. We encourage it at every level to strengthen the quality of care we provide.

  3. I

    Inclusion

    We embrace diversity, creating welcoming environments where differences are respected, barriers are addressed, and everyone can participate and belong.

  4. V

    Value Every Individual

    We recognize the dignity, rights, identity, choices, and potential of every person, keeping them at the centre of decisions affecting their lives.

  5. E

    Excellence

    We deliver safe, compassionate, reliable, person-centred services and value continuous learning, quality improvement, and high standards of practice.

Our values guide every interaction, every decision, and every expression of care.

Our Approach to Service Delivery

Quality care begins with understanding the person receiving it.

  1. Person-Centred Support

    Services shaped around each person’s unique needs, preferences, experiences, and goals, never one-size-fits-all.

  2. Dignity and Respect

    We protect personal dignity and recognize each person’s rights, identity, and choices.

  3. Safety and Well-Being

    Environments and practices that promote physical safety, emotional well-being, and protection from harm.

  4. Independence and Empowerment

    Individuals take part in decisions about their lives and are supported to maintain or develop independence.

  5. Inclusion and Belonging

    We value diversity and promote meaningful participation at home and in the community.

  6. Family and Community Engagement

    Families, caregivers, and community relationships are central to individual well-being.

  7. Quality and Accountability

    Responsible service delivery, professional conduct, continuous learning, and accountability for quality.

Our Community

Rooted in Community. Connected Through Care.

CareVolution Healthcare Inc. is based in Belleville, Ontario, a community known for its local connections, recreational spaces, family activities, and community spirit.

Care does not happen in isolation. It is connected to neighbourhoods, families, community organizations, local resources, and the places where people gather, learn, and participate. Landmarks like the Quinte Sports & Wellness Centre reflect the importance of accessible recreation, wellness, and community participation.

Our commitment is to support individuals not only within their care environments, but in maintaining meaningful connections with the wider community.

The Quinte Sports & Wellness Centre is referenced as a community landmark. This reference does not imply a formal partnership, affiliation, or endorsement.
