Welcome

Welcome to CareHarbour

CareHarbour Canada Inc. is a Canadian health, education, and community services company dedicated to improving the lives of caregivers, individuals, families, and communities.

We develop innovative solutions that combine technology, education, healthcare services, and community partnerships to improve access to care, strengthen caregiver wellness, and support healthier communities.

Whether you are a caregiver, healthcare organization, community agency, or educational institution, CareHarbour is your trusted partner for learning, support, and innovation.

About Us

Who We Are · What We Do · Who We Serve

Who We Are

CareHarbour Canada Inc. is committed to improving health and well-being through innovation, education, collaboration, and community engagement.

Our goal is to build an integrated ecosystem that supports caregivers while helping healthcare organizations improve education, service delivery, and community impact.

Vision

To become Canada's leading provider of innovative health, education, and community care solutions that empower people to live healthier, stronger, and more connected lives.

Mission

To improve lives by providing innovative technology, education, healthcare services, workforce solutions, and community programs that empower caregivers, strengthen organizations, and build healthier communities.

What We Do

One ecosystem, four ways we help

CareHarbour Platform

A digital platform that provides caregiver education, AI-supported caregiving simulations, wellness resources, and community service navigation.

CareHarbour Talk Show

Monthly conversations with healthcare professionals, caregivers, researchers, and community leaders sharing practical knowledge and real-life experiences.

CareHarbour Academy

Professional education, caregiver training, certificate programs, webinars, and continuing education.

Community Partnerships

Connecting caregivers and organizations with trusted community resources, healthcare services, and support programs.
